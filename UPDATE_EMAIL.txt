WHAT THIS IS
A clinical workflow platform built around the four questions we discussed:
1. Who is this?
2. What happened today?
3. What is the workflow?
4. What question are we trying to answer before the next visit?

The system turns every verbal clinical decision into a written schedule and a
dated workflow, then monitors continuity so nothing slips. It flags refills
before they run out, follow-ups that are overdue, tasks that are incomplete,
and prompts that haven't been completed.

IMPORTANT: The AI never prescribes medication and never submits pharmacy
orders. It only recognizes when a refill or new order is needed and hands you
a prompt with all the information you need. You complete the order yourself in
the pharmacy platform, exactly as we designed it.

WHAT YOU'LL SEE
- OVERVIEW — a live status board for every patient: what they're taking now,
  current cycle, when it ends, what comes next, days of medication on hand,
  next order-by date, last meaningful contact, and any open exceptions.
- PATIENTS — full patient list with membership level, treatment phase,
  contact interval, and goals. Click any patient for a detailed view
  (medications, cycle, refill prompts, tasks).
- EXCEPTIONS — the attention list. Run it anytime with the button, and it
  also runs automatically every morning at 06:30 UTC. It flags things like:
  no contact for the expected interval, medication likely to run out within
  two weeks, refill prompt not completed, labs overdue, cycle ending without
  a reassessment, receipt not confirmed, incomplete team tasks.
- REFILL PROMPTS — every generated refill prompt with all 10 fields: patient
  name, DOB, phone, address, email, medication, current dose, quantity
  needed, exhaustion date, order-by date, delivery notes.
- FEEDBACK & INPUT (right-side tab) — this is the important one. If you want
  something changed, added, or fixed, type it here in plain sentences. What
  you submit goes straight into our iteration queue, and we turn it into a
  system update. If a request isn't clear, we'll come back and ask.

ABOUT THE EXCEPTION MONITOR
Each exception appears once and stays until it's resolved — no duplicates.
When a condition clears (patient contacted, refill ordered, task done), the
system marks it resolved automatically. Your morning list will always be
current and actionable.
